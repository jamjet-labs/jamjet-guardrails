# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be civil. Those are compatible, and this project needs
both: the work here is arguing about measurements, and an argument about a
number only works if people can say "this is wrong" plainly and be heard.

Expected:

- Say what you mean. Disagreement about code, a corpus label, or a published
  figure is the point of the project.
- Argue about the claim, not the person who made it.
- Bring evidence. "This number is wrong" is a good comment when it comes with
  the measurement; it is a bad one on its own.
- Accept being wrong without ceremony, and let others do the same. Several of
  the defects recorded in this repository were found by whoever had just
  introduced them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Comments about someone's identity: age, disability, ethnicity, sex, gender
  identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
  orientation.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, code review, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by opening a GitHub security advisory at
<https://github.com/jamjet-labs/jamjet-guardrails/security/advisories/new>. It
is a private channel that reaches the maintainers and nobody else, which is what
matters here; that it is labelled for security is incidental.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will not discuss a report with anyone who
does not need to know about it, including in public threads.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide and act. Depending on what happened, that is a private
word, a public correction, deleting or editing the content, a temporary block,
or a permanent ban from the repository.

We will say what we decided to the person who reported it. We will not always
say why in public, because doing so often means restating the thing that caused
the harm.
